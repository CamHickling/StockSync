StockSync

Count a shelf. See where the systems are wrong.

Recount a shelf in minutes, and catch where your systems drift apart.

  1. 1
    Load your exportsFrom Shopify and Lightspeed. They stay on this computer.
  2. 2
    Scan the shelfAny barcode scanner works, or type the codes.
  3. 3
    Fix what's flaggedThen export the report for whoever updates the systems.

Your Shopify export lists stock at more than one location. The shelf is compared with this location's numbers only.
